## 中文

### 我们的约定

参与本项目（提 Issue、发 PR、参与讨论）的人，应当让这里对所有人都是可参与的，
不因经验水平、国籍、语言、年龄、身份或技术偏好而区别对待。

### 可以接受的

- 就技术方案激烈争论。**这个项目尤其欢迎针对密码学设计和威胁模型的质疑**——
  一个没人挑刺的密码管理器才是危险的
- 直接指出问题，不必迂回
- 用你顺手的语言（中文或英文）

### 不可以接受的

- 人身攻击、羞辱性言论、骚扰
- 基于任何个人特征的歧视性言论
- 未经许可公开他人的私人信息
- 把「直言不讳」当成刻薄的借口。**对事可以尖锐，对人不行。**
- 在公开 Issue 里发布未修复漏洞的利用细节

### 关于批评

作者对本项目的技术批评持开放态度，包括「这个自研格式不该自研」这类根本性质疑。
提出这类意见不会被视为违反本准则。反过来，作者不接受某个技术意见时，
也不构成对提出者的不尊重。

### 执行

违反行为的处理由项目维护者判断，措施从提醒、编辑或删除内容、
到临时或永久禁止参与不等。

举报请发送到 SECURITY.md 里列出的联系邮箱，标题注明「行为准则」。
举报内容会被保密处理。

## English

### Our commitment

Everyone taking part in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions —
should find it a place they can take part in, regardless of experience level,
nationality, language, age, identity or technical preference.

### Acceptable

Vigorous technical disagreement. **Scrutiny of the cryptographic design and
threat model is especially welcome** — a password manager nobody has criticised
is the dangerous kind. Say what you mean directly. Use whichever language you
are comfortable in, Chinese or English.

### Not acceptable

Personal attacks, demeaning remarks, harassment. Discriminatory language based
on any personal characteristic. Publishing others' private information without
permission. Using "bluntness" as cover for cruelty — **be sharp about the work,
not about the person.** Posting exploit details for unfixed vulnerabilities in
public issues.

### On criticism

Fundamental objections — including "this format should not have been
hand-rolled" — are not a code of conduct violation. Nor is the maintainer
declining a technical suggestion a sign of disrespect toward whoever raised it.

### Enforcement

Handled at the maintainer's discretion, ranging from a warning through editing
or removing content to a temporary or permanent ban. Report to the contact
address in SECURITY.md with "Code of Conduct" in the subject. Reports are
handled confidentially.
